02

How leads reach you

How a lead is assigned to you

HubSpot reads the postcode on the enquiry and assigns the rep for that territory. If the postcode doesn't match a territory, the lead goes to Kelly, who passes it on.

The new lead email

When a lead is assigned to you, you get an email with its details and an "Open in HubSpot" link. Kelly receives a copy.

  1. Open the emailIt shows who the lead is and what they enquired about
  2. Click "Open in HubSpot"It takes you straight to the lead record

Returning contacts

When someone already in HubSpot enquires again, you get a new "Re-attempting" lead for them. Before you call, check their history on the contact record: earlier enquiries, calls and deals.

When a colleague has left

Their contacts are re-routed by postcode when they enquire again, so they reach the rep who covers that territory now.

Enquiries that are not yours

Home users, international enquiries and Partner Programme sign-ups don't go to field reps. If one reaches you by mistake, let Kelly know.

04

Working a new lead

Review a lead before you call

On the lead record you'll find the enquiry form, the device they're interested in, the source, the lead type and the company. Check previous activity too, so you know whether you're talking to someone new.

Make first contact within 24 hours

Call, email or WhatsApp from the lead record, and log it so it counts. See Logging activity.

05

Qualifying or closing a lead

Is this a real buyer?

Coming soonThe qualification checklist, agreed with Kelly

Qualify a lead

Move the lead to Qualified. The contact becomes a sales qualified lead and HubSpot creates the deal for you.

Disqualify a lead with a reason

Move the lead to Disqualified, pick the reason and add a note. Reasons include no interest, bad timing, home user, distributor territory and Academy access.

Coming soonLeads with no movement for a set number of days will be disqualified automatically as "no response"

10

Help

I didn't get the email

Open the New leads view: the lead is there even if the email didn't arrive. Let Kelly know so it can be checked.

The lead isn't in my view

Check that you're the lead owner and that the view's filters haven't been changed.

This lead belongs to another territory

Don't work it. Let Kelly know so it's reassigned.

The contact is owned by someone else

Work your lead as normal, and ask Kelly before changing the contact owner.
