Where borrowers drop off

App onboarding drop-off, based on last_onboarding_step (last completed step). Live HubSpot data.

1. Main enrollment funnel

Bar = contacts who reached each step (cumulative). "N here" = contacts whose last completed step is exactly this one; click it to open that segment in HubSpot.

On the payment page — not paid yet (0)

Contacts whose last completed step is terms: they accepted the ToS and are sitting on the payment screen without paying. Click any to open the record in HubSpot — this is the list for the payment drop-off automation.

2. Funnel by vertical

Pick an employer_type to open its funnel — the other cards collapse, and ← brings them back. "No employer type" = early-stage contacts who have not entered an employer yet.

3. Funnel by employer

All employers with at least 1 contact, ranked by volume. Click a row for its stage funnel. Names via Companies object; test clients excluded.

4. Funnel by broker

All brokers with at least 1 contact, ranked by volume. Click a row for its stage funnel. Names via Companies broker_name (a broker_id can map to several agents; most frequent shown).
