About this guide

This guide shows how leads and deals work in Dermalux's HubSpot, from the moment someone enquires until you create the deal and work it to close. Each section has the steps and, where there is one, a short video. Sections marked "Not live yet" are still being set up and will be filled in as they go live. If something here doesn't match what you see in HubSpot, let Kelly know.

How leads reach you

How a lead is assigned to you

When a professional enquires, HubSpot uses the postcode to pick the territory and gives the contact to that territory's rep. You get a lead at the New stage with the label HOT.

TerritoryRep
Midlands & NorthDyan Williams. Liverpool, Manchester, Warrington, Oldham and Chester postcodes go to Katie Greenwood.
North & ScotlandKatie Greenwood
South WestJoanne Briggs
South EastStacey King
LondonAndrea Dunne

No postcode matchKelly Wiseman, who checks the postcode and reassigns the lead.

The new lead email

You get an email titled "New lead assigned" with the lead's name, the dates, the lead type and the label. Kelly gets a copy.

1Open the email
It shows who the lead is and when it was assigned.
2Open the lead in HubSpot
Use the link in the email, or find it in your New leads view.

Returning contacts

When someone already in HubSpot enquires again, you get a new lead for them. The end of the lead name tells you what kind it is.

Lead name ends inWhat it means
"- Re-attempting"A contact who enquired before. Check their earlier enquiries and calls first.
"- Upsell"An existing customer. Check which devices they already have.
Just the nameNew business from a contact already in HubSpot.

If the contact already has an open lead or deal, HubSpot doesn't create another one.

When a colleague has left

If a contact owned by someone who has left enquires again, HubSpot reassigns them by postcode to whoever covers that territory now.

Enquiries that are not yours

Home users go to Internal Sales and international enquiries go to Jane Annable. If a home user later says they're buying for a clinic or salon, HubSpot re-routes them to the field rep for their territory. If one reaches you by mistake, let Kelly know.

IV

Working a new lead

Review a lead before you call

On the lead record you'll find the enquiry form, the device they're interested in, the source, the lead type and the company. Check past activity too.

Make first contact within 24 hours

Call, email or WhatsApp from the lead record. Log it, or the lead won't move to Attempting (see Logging your activity).

Lead stages

Qualifying or closing a lead

Is this a real buyer?

Not live yet
The qualification checklist, agreed with Kelly.

Qualify a lead

When it's a real buyer, create a deal from the lead record. HubSpot then moves the lead to Qualified and the contact becomes a sales qualified lead.

Not live yet
HubSpot will create the deal for you when you move a lead to Qualified. Until then, create the deal yourself.

Disqualify a lead with a reason

Move the lead to Disqualified, pick the reason and add a note. Reasons include no interest, bad timing, home user, distributor territory, Academy access and no response in nurture.

Not live yet
Leads with no movement for a set number of days will be disqualified automatically as "no response".

Help

1I didn't get the email
Open the New leads view: the lead is there even if the email didn't arrive. Let Kelly know.
2The lead isn't in my view
Check that you're the lead owner and that the view's filters haven't been changed.
3This lead belongs to another territory
Don't work it. Let Kelly know so it's reassigned.
4The contact is owned by someone else
Work your lead as normal and ask Kelly before changing the contact owner.
